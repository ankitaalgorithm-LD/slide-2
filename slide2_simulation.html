What is a feasibility check?

A feasibility check helps determine three things before a request moves forward.
1

Can it be supported?

Whether the requirement can be supported at all.

2

Existing capability?

Whether an existing capability can meet the requirement.

3

Extra support needed?

Whether additional technical or product support is needed.
